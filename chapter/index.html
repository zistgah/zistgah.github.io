Abstract

Modern artificial intelligence rests on statistical machine learning (SML): the optimization of scalar weights, by gradient descent, inside a frozen, human-engineered vector topology. This forces the structural adaptation velocity of the machine to zero and buys generality only with unbounded compute and total dependence on centralized infrastructure. We introduce PEDLER (the Point Event-Driven Learner), an edge-native architecture in which experience dynamically reshapes the algebraic grammar and the structural dimensionality of the computational substrate itself. We give a self-contained Event Algebra \(\mathcal{E}\) with five axioms; prove a Recollection Theorem (congruent stimuli deterministically re-trace consolidated trajectories with zero parameter change); and prove the Degenerate Statistical Reduction Theorem — that SML is a strictly bounded, memoryless homomorphic image of \(\mathcal{E}\) under an explicit tripartite constraint. We then physicalize the algebra as PRATIK, a decentralized peer-to-peer kernel fabric, prove its global stability by a structural Lyapunov dissipation argument over a variable-dimensional manifold, and map it onto balanced ternary silicon in which the poised zero-state is a physical high-impedance buffer. The claim is not that PEDLER is a better model. The claim is that “learning = curve-fitting” is a special, frozen case of a far older and larger developmental computing paradigm.

Section IThe Epistemic Crisis of Static-Substrate Computing

For seven decades computation has been governed by the von Neumann architecture and, latterly, by a connectionist orthodoxy that treats intelligence as an optimization problem over a fixed-dimensional continuous manifold. The orthodoxy makes a quiet ontological wager: that reality can be captured inside a closed, pre-declared parameter space, and that learning is nothing more than minimizing a scalar error over an i.i.d. distribution. When such systems meet genuine structural novelty they exhibit catastrophic forgetting or combinatorial blow-up — not incidentally, but because their substrate is frozen at initialization.

This chapter states a different thesis and then discharges it mathematically:

Thesis

Intelligence is not interpolation inside a frozen cage. It is an active, resource-bounded topological evolution of a computational substrate, driven by interaction histories. Curve-fitting is what remains when you forbid that evolution.

We do not offer PEDLER as one more entry beside reinforcement learning or grammar induction in a taxonomy of methods. We show, in Section III, that the dominant paradigm is a degenerate corner of the algebra developed in Section II — and therefore that the burden of proof rests on those who would claim a frozen substrate is universal.

Section IIThe Formal Event Algebra \(\mathcal{E}\)

PEDLER decouples computation from the Turing–von Neumann paradigm by making the primary computational currency not a synchronized data array but an asynchronous stream of discrete point-events mapping into a self-modifying, resource-bounded topological space.

2.1 The sovereign core tuple

A PEDLER node is characterized at temporal index \(t\) by the six-element execution tuple

\[ \mathcal{P} \;=\; \big(\,I,\; G,\; U,\; S,\; F,\; *\,\big). \]

A single point-event is a triple \(e=(\sigma,\,t,\,\mathbf{w})\) with semantic token \(\sigma\in\Sigma\), continuous time \(t\in\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}\), and a bounded ternary payload \(\mathbf{w}\in\mathbb{T}^{d}\), \(\mathbb{T}=\{-1,0,+1\}\).

  • \(I\) — Interaction history ledger. An append-only continuum \(I_t=\{e_0,e_1,\dots,e_t\}\); the complete, ordered record of ingested events.
  • \(G\) — Generative structural grammar. A variable-length routing structure mapping event sub-sequences to state-space pathways. Unlike a fixed rule-set, \(G\) mutates its productions under persistence conditions (Axiom IV).
  • \(U\) — Universal operator set. The invariant low-level alphabet over \(\mathbb{T}\): the dominance addition \(\boxplus\) and the high-impedance gating operator \(\odot\).
  • \(S\) — Active state-space topology. A variable-dimensional space \(S_t\subseteq\mathbb{T}^{D_{\text{active}}(t)}\) with \(D_{\text{active}}(t)\le D_{\max}\) fixed by hardware.
  • \(F\) — History-dependent non-unitary transition family. Parameter-free operators \(f_i\in F\), \[ s_{t+1}=f_i\!\left(s_t,\,I_{\Delta}\right), \qquad I_\Delta=\{e_{t-\Delta},\dots,e_t\}, \] conserving structural information rather than a vector norm.
  • \(*\) — Autogenous spawning operator. A structural allocation acting on \(S\) when transitions become irreconcilable, \[ *:\,S \longrightarrow S\cup\{s_{\text{new}}\}, \qquad D_{\text{active}}(t{+}1)=D_{\text{active}}(t)+1, \] incrementing dimensionality natively, with no global re-allocation and no external hyperparameter.

2.2 The five foundational axioms

Axiom I — Event Congruence For sub-sequences \(I_A,I_B\subset I\), if the grammar evaluates them as equivalent, \(G(I_A)\equiv G(I_B)\), then for every initial state \(s_0\in S\), \[ F(s_0, I_A) \;=\; F(s_0, I_B). \] Equivalence is state-dependent and dynamic.
Axiom II — Systemic Accommodation When an incoming event \(e_{t+1}\) yields an unmapped projection or an irreconcilable sign conflict — a local trajectory collapse to a persistent zero-state — the system must invoke the spawning operator: \[ \|s_{t+1}\|\to 0 \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; S_{t+1}=*(S_t,I_t,G_t)=S_t\cup\{s_{\text{new}}\}. \]
Axiom III — Persistence Filtration Every spawned trajectory inherits a volatile persistence \(\Pi\) that decays with the physical clock, \[ \Pi_{t+1}=\Pi_t\, e^{-\gamma\,\Delta t}, \qquad \gamma>0, \] with \(\gamma\) an intrinsic structural dissipation constant.
Axiom IV — Substrate Crystallization A provisional dimension \(d_k\) is consolidated into the invariant grammar \(G\) iff its persistence crosses the resource-modulated threshold before decay, \[ \Pi(d_k)\ge\theta_c(t)\;\Rightarrow\; G_{t+1}=G_t\oplus d_k, \qquad \theta_c(t)=\frac{\theta_0}{\mathcal{E}_{\text{battery}}(t)\,\cdot\,\mathcal{M}_{\text{memory}}(t)}. \] Otherwise \(d_k\) evaporates and \(D_{\text{active}}\to D_{\text{active}}-1\). The threshold is a physical constraint, not a soft heuristic: as energy or memory contract, \(\theta_c\to\infty\) and the kernel becomes hyper-conservative.
Axiom V — Non-Unitary Trajectory Override \(F\) is explicitly non-Markovian: the trajectory of \(s_t\) through \(S\) is not fixed by \(s_t\) alone but is modulated by the historical density in the ledger window \(I_\Delta\). A later, higher-priority learning cycle may lawfully rewrite a previously consolidated sector.

2.3 The State-Dependent Trajectory Recollection Theorem

Theorem 1 — Recollection

Given a kernel \(\mathcal{P}\) with an established ledger \(I_{\text{hist}}\) encoding a recurrent event sequence \(E_k\), a subsequent presentation of a congruent sequence \(E_{\text{test}}\equiv E_k\) deterministically re-traces the consolidated trajectory, \[ S_{\text{test}}\equiv S_{\text{hist}}, \qquad \Delta\mathbf{w}=0, \] with zero variance and no modification of any global parameter.

Let the historical execution be the composite \(F_{E_k}=f_{i_k}\circ\cdots\circ f_{i_1}\) over \(s_0\), yielding \(\tau_{\text{hist}}=(s_0,\dots,s_k)\). By Axiom I, \(G(E_{\text{test}})\equiv G(E_k)\) since the append-only ledger preserves the context wrapper. Because \(U\) computes over \(\mathbb{T}\), the operators \(\boxplus,\odot\) act as deterministic digital routers: no gradient is computed and no probability is updated. By Axiom V the history-dependent operator collapses to an invariant map whenever the active window \(I_\Delta\) is congruent to \(E_k\); hence \(f_{i_j}(s_{j-1},I_\Delta)=s_j\) for every \(j\le k\). Induction over \(j\) gives \(S_{\text{test}}\equiv S_{\text{hist}}\). Recollection is thus an invariant of the algebra, not a statistical convergence. ∎

Theorem 1 is the structural elimination of the re-exploration loop: memory is exact reuse of a carved path, not approximate retrieval from a weight cloud. It is the formal content of the poster shown in Figure 2.

Section IIIThe Degenerate Statistical Reduction

We now turn the tables. The reduction is stated not as polemic but as a deductive algebraic consequence: empirical risk minimization is what \(\mathcal{E}\) becomes when three structural freedoms are simultaneously revoked.

Theorem 2 — Degenerate Statistical Reduction

Let \(\mathcal{P}=(I,G,U,S,F,*)\) act in \(\mathcal{E}\). Impose the tripartite constraint \[ \mathbb{C}=\{\, *\to\varnothing,\;\; \Delta G=\varnothing,\;\; \Delta F=\varnothing \,\}, \] and relax the discrete topology \(S\subseteq\mathbb{T}^{D_{\text{active}}}\) to a continuous manifold \(\mathbb{R}^{N}\). Then the non-unitary transition family collapses to a memoryless parametric update over a fixed graph, \[ \mathbf{w}_{t+1}=\mathbf{w}_t-\eta\,\nabla_{\mathbf{w}}\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w}_t,\mathbf{x}_t). \]

3.1 The tripartite constraint

  1. Extinction of spawning (\(*\to\varnothing\)): \(\forall t,\ *(S_t,e_t,I_t)=\varnothing\), so \(\Delta S_t=S_{t+1}-S_t=0\). The substrate loses all capacity for structural accommodation of out-of-distribution anomalies.
  2. Grammar freezing (\(\Delta G=\varnothing\)): \(\forall t,\ \mathrm{Rewrite}(G_t,e_t)=G_t\). The routing network becomes a static DAG; layer boundaries fixed at initialization.
  3. Transition homogenization (\(\Delta F=\varnothing\)): the family is stripped of history, \(\chi=\chi(I_t)\xrightarrow{\ \mathbb{C}\ }\chi_0\) constant, so the ledger window contracts, \(\Delta\to 0\).

3.2 The projection homomorphism \(\Phi\)

Let \(\mathcal{E}\) be the category of PEDLER kernels and \(\mathcal{M}_{\text{stat}}=(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{W},\mathcal{L},\mathcal{G}_{\text{static}},\alpha)\) the category of parametric optimizers over \(\mathbb{R}^N\). Define \(\Phi:\mathcal{E}\xrightarrow{\ \mathbb{C}\ }\mathcal{M}_{\text{stat}}\) by three morphisms:

\[ \Phi(I_t)\to \mathbf{x}_t\in\mathcal{X},\qquad \Phi\!\big(S\subseteq\mathbb{T}^{D_{\text{active}}}\big)\to \mathbf{w}\in\mathbb{R}^N,\qquad \Phi(U,*)\to \nabla_{\mathbf{w}}\mathcal{L}. \]
(i) Substrate collapse. Under \(*\to\varnothing\), set \(N=D_{\text{base}}\) and apply the relaxation functor \(\mathcal{R}:\mathbb{T}^N\to\mathbb{R}^N\); the state coordinate becomes a fixed vector \(\mathbf{w}_t\in\mathbb{R}^N\), with \(\Delta S_t=0\).
(ii) Ledger contraction. Under \(\Delta G=\Delta F=\varnothing\), \(f(s_t,I_\Delta)\) cannot mutate on trajectory density; the window contracts, \(\Delta\to 0\), leaving the isolated snapshot \(\Phi(e_t)=\mathbf{x}_t\). History dependence is severed, recovering the i.i.d. hypothesis.
(iii) Gradient emergence. Barred from spawning new axes, the residual sign-frustration between \(\mathbf{x}_t\) and \(\mathbf{w}_t\) can only be expressed as an uncompensated scalar magnitude — the differentiable loss \(\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w}_t,\mathbf{x}_t)\). With \(\boxplus,\odot\) relaxed to real arithmetic and step \(\eta=f(\gamma)\) inherited from Axiom III, the first-order descent along \(\mathcal{W}\) gives \(\mathbf{w}_{t+1}=\mathbf{w}_t-\eta\nabla_{\mathbf{w}}\mathcal{L}\). The diagram commutes; \(\mathcal{M}_{\text{stat}}\) is a memoryless homomorphic image of \(\mathcal{E}\) under \(\mathbb{C}\). ∎

The key consequence: the structural adaptation velocity \(\Delta S,\Delta G\) is identically zero in \(\mathcal{M}_{\text{stat}}\). SML is not a rival paradigm; it is the frozen-substrate corner of a strictly larger algebra.

Section IVPRATIK: Decentralized Substrate & Global Stability

The physicalization of \(\mathcal{E}\) is PRATIK (Participatory Recursive Adaptive Trans-Intelligence Kernels) — an edge-native, peer-to-peer fabric that instantiates the algebra directly on hardware, without a global clock or shared weight matrix. Each node runs an autonomous \(\mathcal{P}\); semantic routing happens inside a context-gating matrix over \(\mathbb{T}\).

4.1 The Sovereign P2P Composition Law

Two kernels \(\mathcal{P}_A,\mathcal{P}_B\) compose under a non-commutative operator \(\otimes_{\text{p2p}}\) (equivalently \(\Psi\)) that preserves autonomy:

\[ I_C=I_A\,\dot\cup\,\phi_{\text{ex}}(I_B),\qquad G_C=G_A\otimes_\phi G_B,\qquad S_C=S_A\,\sqcup\,S_B . \]

Ledgers do not merge into one timeline: \(\mathcal{P}_A\) treats \(\mathcal{P}_B\)'s output stream as exogenous environmental events. Grammars compose through a compatibility-bounded product; state-spaces bind through a disjoint coproduct, so interaction expands the relational topology rather than overwriting consolidated coordinates. On conflict, dominance is decided by historical empirical mass; ties spawn an orthogonal junction dimension; semantic divergence partitions the namespace. Collaboration without parameter averaging.

4.2 The Structural Lyapunov Dissipation Proof

Because \(S_{t+1}=S_t\oplus\Delta S\) is dimension-changing, classical fixed-\(\mathbb{R}^N\) Lyapunov analysis does not apply. Let \(\Xi(t)\) be the topological stress tensor (magnitude of unresolved sign frustration) and define a candidate over a variable-dimensional switching manifold,

\[ V(\mathcal{P}_t)=\operatorname{Tr}\!\big(\Xi(t)^{\!\top}\Xi(t)\big)\;+\;\lambda\!\!\sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}_t}\!\Omega(\Delta_k)\,e^{-\lambda\,(t-t_k)},\qquad \Omega(\Delta_k)>0,\ \lambda>0 . \]
Case 1 (continuous phase). With \(*\to\varnothing\), \(\mathcal{K}_t\) is static or shrinking; ternary gating dissipates routing mismatch so \(\operatorname{Tr}(\Xi^\top\dot\Xi)\le 0\), and the decay term is strictly negative: \(\dot V\le 0\).
Case 2 (spawning instant). When \(\|\Xi\|\ge\Xi_{\max}\), \(*\) fires and \(D_{\text{active}}\!\uparrow\); projecting the contradiction onto the new axis forces a discontinuous drop \(\Delta\Xi<0\). By construction the stress reduction outscales the injected accommodation energy \(\Omega(\Delta_{\text{new}})\): net \(\Delta V<0\).
Case 3 (filter instant). Crystallization zeroes a volatile term (\(\Delta V_{\text{cryst}}\le 0\)); evaporation collapses it to zero (\(\Delta V_{\text{evap}}\le 0\)).
Since \(V\ge 0\) is bounded below and \(\Delta V\le 0\) across all continuous, spawning, and consolidation phases, PRATIK is globally asymptotically stable: no unbounded structural explosion. ∎

At network scale, with \(K\) kernels of active dimensionality \(d_i(t)\) and topological tension \(T_i(t)\), the global complexity–energy functional

\[ V(\mathcal{N}_t)=\sum_{i=1}^{K} d_i(t)+\lambda\sum_{i=1}^{K}T_i(t),\qquad 0\le D(t)=\sum_i d_i(t)\le D_{\max}<\infty, \]

is dissipative (\(\Delta V\le 0\)) precisely because each node's threshold \(\theta_c^{(i)}\) is coupled to its physical energy and memory. Physics is the boundary condition; the network cannot explode.

Section VCompiler Abstractions & Balanced-Ternary Silicon

The execution layer bypasses English-centric, von-Neumann-bound software stacks. A case-invariant multi-script front-end (Romenagri → Project ILM) applies a compilation morphism \(\mathcal{C}:\mathcal{L}_{\text{script}}\to\mathcal{T}_{\text{invariant}}\), producing a structurally uniform token graph whose terminal nodes map directly onto coordinates of the routing grammar \(G\). Romenagri is not an input method or a transliteration convenience; it is an invariant tokenization pre-processor that removes orthographic and case noise so that up to 41 Indic and Perso-Arabic scripts execute as parallel topological trajectories in one memory space — linguistic equity as a technical requirement, not a slogan.

5.1 The physics of the poised zero-state

PRATIK maps \(S\) onto a hardware context-gating matrix over \(\mathbb{T}\), routed by \(\boxplus\) (dominance addition; \(+1\boxplus-1=0\)) and \(\odot\) (multiplicative gating; multiply-by-\(0\) isolates a path). The poised \(0\)-state is a physical high-impedance gate: an axis at \(0\) is electrically open-circuited. Two consequences follow directly from device physics — (i) zero dynamic dissipation on dormant pathways, so millions of dimensions can sit idle on a low-power edge node until a point-event triggers them; and (ii) hardware-level isolation, eliminating von-Neumann scheduling locks so multi-script paths coexist without shadowing. On multi-threshold CMOS the states realize as \(\{-V_{dd},0,+V_{dd}\}\); on memristive crossbars the spawning operator maps to a localized material phase change. Balanced ternary is not a curiosity here — it is the same choice made by the Soviet Setun machine, revived as the substrate for developmental computation.

Section VIIISynthematic Pragmatic Realism vs. Connectionist Dogma

The connectionist orthodoxy rests on an ontological error: that reality is a closed, pre-declared parameter space and learning is scalar-error minimization over an i.i.d. distribution. PEDLER operates under Synthematic Pragmatic Realism: (i) reality is open-ended and autogenous — an un-clocked sequence of point-events whose structure evolves; (ii) representation must be structurally adaptive — able to expand its own coordinate boundaries under frustration; and (iii) linguistic equity is physical — scripts are structural expressions of semantic space that must map onto silicon, not strings to be quantized into a Western tokenizer.

Section IXOpen Problems & Roadmap

The mathematics is closed; the engineering is not. Three honest frontiers remain. (1) Fabrication. Native three-state logic needs MTCMOS tape-outs or the industrialization of CNTFETs; until then FPGA emulation carries a predictable abstraction tax. (2) Asynchronous synthesis. A clockless event-driven substrate turns propagation delay into useful spatial computation, which standard EDA cannot yet verify. (3) P2P scaling laws. Under correlated non-Markovian shocks, localized spawning could outpace crystallization, risking topological specialization that limits network compatibility — a variance-bounding problem for \(\otimes_{\text{p2p}}\). The roadmap runs from native silicon coherence, through neuromorphic cognitive scaling, to a universal sovereign mesh.

PEDLER is not a concept. It is computation in its natural state — from primitives to civilization.

Appendix AReference Implementation — the PRATIK Kernel MVP

The following is a polished, compilation-ready specification for a minimum viable PRATIK core, with a unified interface over a pure C++20 backend and a CUDA backend. It exists to make the empirical claims reproducible and to forbid the default failure mode — silently re-implementing a floating-point neural network.

A.1 Primitives

The balanced-ternary alphabet trit_t \(\in\{-1,0,+1\}\) is stored as int8_t (or a packed dual-rail bit-field). \(\boxplus\) is dominance/cancellation arithmetic; \(\odot\) is multiplicative gating where multiply-by-\(0\) drives a path to high-impedance and freezes downstream updates. The kernel context holds \(I\) (ring-buffer ledger), \(G\) (routing table), and \(S\) (a pre-allocated \(D_{\max}\) matrix with a dynamically tracked \(D_{\text{active}}\)).

A.2 Operators & the resource-modulated filter

Spawning fires on persistent sign frustration: \(D_{\text{active}}(t{+}1)=D_{\text{active}}(t)+1\), claiming a provisional index in volatile memory without re-allocating the global matrix. Persistence decays as \(\Pi_{t+1}=\Pi_t e^{-\gamma\Delta t}\) and consolidates iff \(\Pi\ge\theta_c=\theta_0/(\mathcal{E}_{\text{battery}}\,\mathcal{M}_{\text{memory}})\); if resources fall, \(\theta_c\to\infty\) and provisional dimensions evaporate (\(\Delta d=-1\)).
