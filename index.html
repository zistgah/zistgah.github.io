Zistgah

A new habitat for humanity… for the AGI era.

Abhishek Choudhary · AyeAI · Built in Indian thought. For the world.

A habitat is not a shelter — it is everything a life needs to flourish: a language, a ground, a mind, a way of building together, and room to grow beyond the horizon. Zistgah is that habitat being built in the open, for the era in which humans and AGI live and work together. It subsumes Mangal Kamna — open-science space exploration for energy and resources — and OASIS — open lunar and interplanetary settlement. But charity begins at home: the habitat starts here, with working tools anyone can use today, each one verified by execution and citable by DOI.

Build it with us

The habitat is built in the open, issue by issue. Found a defect? Have an idea — a tool, a domain, a language, an experiment? Want to contribute a build? Open an issue: it lands in a scrum-style backlog, gets sized into a short increment, and is built under the verification gate — with your issue as its recorded intent.
